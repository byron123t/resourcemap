    Read with care. Totals add up only what each site reports; many large oil fields, including Saudi Arabia's, publish no production. The US critical-mineral set is far more detailed than the global one, so the US shows many more deposits. Solar, wind, hydro and nuclear come from a 2021 database. Oil refineries aren't included yet. Gas is converted at 6,000 cf per barrel of oil equivalent. Country totals count operating sites only; offshore sites go to the nearest country. Country water stress is an area-weighted average of Aqueduct basin scores. Borders follow Natural Earth's de facto lines and don't imply endorsement.
