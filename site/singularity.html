SINGULARITY

You run an AI company. Your goal: automate every job on Earth. Compute is everything: it needs chips from a handful of real fabs and chokepoints, grid power from real plants, and cash from investors who only fund the leader. Every capability you gain makes the world more nervous, and if governments agree a Global AI Pause Treaty, you're finished. So are you if you run out of money, or burn through the world's spare fuel and minerals before reaching superintelligence.

Choose your approach
