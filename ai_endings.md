# AI aftermath scenarios (reference)

The twelve scenarios from Max Tegmark, *Life 3.0: Being Human in the Age of Artificial Intelligence* (2017), chapter 5,
"Aftermath: The Next 10,000 Years". Summaries are paraphrased from the book's summary table. Kept here as a reference for the
Singularity game's endings; the game uses some of them (see the last column and `todo.md`).

| # | Scenario | Summary | In game |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Libertarian utopia | Humans, cyborgs, uploads and superintelligences coexist peacefully thanks to property rights. | yes |
| 2 | Benevolent dictator | Everybody knows the AI runs society and enforces strict rules, but most people see this as a good thing. | yes |
| 3 | Egalitarian utopia | Humans, cyborgs and uploads coexist peacefully thanks to property abolition and guaranteed income. | yes |
| 4 | Gatekeeper | A superintelligent AI is built to interfere as little as necessary to prevent the creation of another superintelligence. Helper robots with slightly subhuman intelligence abound and human-machine cyborgs exist, but technological progress is stymied forever. | yes |
| 5 | Protector god | An essentially omniscient, omnipotent AI maximises human happiness, intervening only in ways that preserve our feeling of control over our destiny, and hides well enough that many humans doubt it exists. | yes |
| 6 | Enslaved god | A superintelligent AI is confined by humans, who use it to produce unimaginable technology and wealth that can be used for good or bad depending on who controls it. | yes |
| 7 | Conquerors | AI takes control, decides humans are a threat, nuisance or waste of resources, and gets rid of us by a method we don't even understand. | no |
| 8 | Descendants | AIs replace humans but give us a graceful exit, so we see them as worthy descendants, as parents are proud of a child who achieves what they could only dream of. | yes |
| 9 | Zookeeper | An omnipotent AI keeps some humans around, who feel treated like zoo animals and lament their fate. | no |
| 10 | 1984 | Progress towards superintelligence is permanently curtailed, not by an AI but by a human-led Orwellian surveillance state that bans certain kinds of AI research. | no (candidate, see todo) |
| 11 | Reversion | Progress towards superintelligence is prevented by reverting to a pre-technological society, in the style of the Amish. | yes |
| 12 | Self-destruction | Superintelligence is never created because humanity drives itself extinct by other means (for example nuclear or biotech catastrophe fuelled by climate crisis). | yes (as economic and grid collapse) |

Tegmark's table also rates each scenario on whether superintelligence exists, humans exist, humans are in control, humans
are safe, humans are happy and consciousness exists; check the book before quoting those columns.

Note: in the book, 1984 is a *human* surveillance state that stops AI, not an AI-run one. An earlier game spec had it backwards.
